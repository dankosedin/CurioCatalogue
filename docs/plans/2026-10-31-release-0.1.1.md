## How to work on this plan

The steps are sized for one short session each, often with Sonnet: the project skill `/step <id>` (`.claude/skills/step/`)
holds the session protocol. Each step says:
- **Branch:** where the work goes. After the plan's own pull request is merged, new branches start from `main`.
- **After:** steps or merges that must come first.
- **Model:** `sonnet` for well-defined work; `opus` where design or a judgement is the work.
- **Author:** what needs the author's decision before or during the step; "none" means the session goes ahead on its own.
- **Done when:** what must be true before the step is ticked.

A step with an `Author:` decision asks first and does not guess. A session that stops in the middle writes one line under
"Where we stopped" at the end of this plan.
